Who We Are

Lima Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration company based at 2400 Elida Rd in Lima, Ohio. We handle emergency extraction, structural drying and the repairs that follow for homes and businesses in Allen County and the nearby towns of Spencerville, Wapakoneta, Delphos, Ada and Ottawa.

We are licensed and insured. We do not list licence numbers or carrier names on this site, and we are happy to talk through what our coverage includes when you call.

How We Work

  1. Call the 24/7 line and tell us what is wet.
  2. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show where the water went.
  3. Pumps pull out standing water, and ruined materials come out with it.
  4. Drying equipment stays on site and we log readings every day.
  5. A written repair scope lays out the rebuild before any work begins.

What You Can Expect From Us

We explain the scope and the price before work starts, and we explain what could change them. We share photographs and readings, because they are your records as much as ours.

We do not promise arrival times, and we do not make promises about what your insurer will pay. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and your insurer decides coverage.

What We Do Not Do

We do not do mold remediation, basement waterproofing or foundation repair. When a job needs one of those trades, we say so and point you toward the right specialist.

We also do not guess. If we cannot see the property, we cannot give you a firm number, and we will tell you that plainly.
