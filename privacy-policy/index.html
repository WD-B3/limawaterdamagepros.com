Overview

This policy explains how Lima Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website, call our line or email us. This site has no forms, so we do not collect information through web forms.

Information We Collect

When you call, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the water damage. When you email, we receive your email address and what you write.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call you agree to this recording.

How We Use Information

We use the information to respond to your request, schedule and perform work, document the loss and communicate about your job. We may share information with subcontractors and insurers when you ask us to or when it is needed to do the work.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third Party Services On This Site

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.

Your browser may also store small files to make the site work. You can clear these in your browser settings.

Retention And Security

We keep job records for as long as needed to serve you, meet legal and insurance requirements and resolve disputes. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your Choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you, subject to legal and business record requirements. Contact us at contact@limawaterdamagepros.com.

Changes And Contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions can go to contact@limawaterdamagepros.com or by mail to Lima Water Damage Pros, 2400 Elida Rd, Lima, OH 45805.
