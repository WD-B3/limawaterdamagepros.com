A burst pipe is an emergency measured in minutes. Pressurized supply lines keep pushing water until someone closes a valve, which is why burst pipe water damage in Lima often reaches the ceiling below, the walls beside it and the subfloor under it before the leak is even found. The 24/7 line is open for exactly this call.

First, close the main shut-off valve, which is usually where the water line enters the house near the meter or the water heater. Then open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the line, switch off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry, and move valuables out of the path of the water.

Burst Pipe Emergency: What To Do First

Speed matters more here than on almost any other loss, because the water source is under pressure. Shutting the main valve cuts the flow, and draining the lines reduces what is left to escape.

Take photos of the damage and the broken pipe before anything is moved. Do not walk through standing water to reach a panel or outlet, and call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk before you call us.

  1. Close the main water valve.
  2. Open the lowest faucet to drain the line.
  3. Photograph the break and the wet areas.
  4. Lift valuables off the floor and out of wet cabinets.
  5. Call the emergency line for extraction and drying.

Why Supply Line Failures Cause So Much Damage

A supply line carries water at household pressure, which is far more force than a drain or a roof leak. A split in a half-inch line can release a great deal of water in an hour, and a slow pinhole can wet a wall cavity for weeks without any visible stain.

Pipes fail at joints, at old fittings and at spots where they were stressed by cold or by water hammer. Pipes inside exterior walls and unheated spaces are the most common failures in Lima after a hard freeze, which is covered on our frozen pipe damage page.

How The Cleanup Works

After the water is stopped, the job follows a fixed order so nothing wet gets left behind.

Extract

Standing water is pumped and vacuumed out. See water extraction for the equipment.

Map

Moisture meters and thermal imaging find water inside walls, under flooring and above ceilings.

Open and dry

Wet insulation and soaked drywall are removed where needed, and air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings are dry.

Repair

Drywall, paint, flooring and trim are replaced after drying, as scoped in advance.

Hidden Water Behind Walls And Under Sinks

A burst line under a sink or in a wall often does more damage than it looks like from the room. Water runs down the inside of the wall to the floor plate, then spreads under cabinets and baseboards.

If the break was in a kitchen, our kitchen water damage page explains how cabinet bases are dried. If the wall itself is wet, the wall and drywall repair page covers cutting and drying wall cavities.

Burst Pipes In Lima Homes

The 1991 to 2020 climate normals put the average January low in Lima near 19 degrees Fahrenheit, and a hard freeze can split lines that held up through milder winters. Supply lines in exterior walls, garages and crawl spaces are the first to go.

If you are away in winter, set the heat no lower than a level that keeps pipes warm and ask a neighbor to check the house. A burst line in an empty home runs for days, and insurers often look closely at whether the property was kept heated.

What Affects The Cost Of Burst Pipe Cleanup

No two burst pipe losses are alike. The main variables are how long the water ran and what it reached.

How long it ran

A leak caught in minutes soaks one room, while one that ran overnight can reach every floor below.

Rooms and floors affected

The count of rooms drives equipment and labor, and water traveling through ceilings adds repair scope.

Materials wet

Insulation, drywall and hardwood respond differently, and some must be replaced.

Drying days

Dense framing and subfloors need more monitored days than carpet.

Rebuild scope

Replacement of drywall, paint and flooring is priced after drying is complete.

Sudden pipe bursts are often covered by homeowners policies, while long-term seepage is often excluded, so how quickly you report matters. Check your policy and call your insurer early.

Common Questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Lima?

Yes. The emergency line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Shut the main valve first, then call so the crew can head out prepared.

Should I repair the broken pipe before you start?

The pipe needs a plumber. Water damage crews handle extraction and drying, and a plumber repairs the supply line. Coordinate so the leak is stopped before drying starts.

How do I know water is inside the walls?

Look for stains, bubbling paint, a musty odor, or a baseboard that has lifted. A moisture meter and thermal camera confirm it without cutting anything open.

Does my insurance cover a burst pipe?

Many homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental pipe bursts but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself. Read your policy or ask your insurer which parts apply.

How long until the house is dry?

Most burst pipe losses need several days of monitored drying. Dry readings, not the calendar, decide when equipment is removed.
