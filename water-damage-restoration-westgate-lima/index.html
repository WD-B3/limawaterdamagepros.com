Westgate is the name used for the west side of Lima around the Route 81 corridor, where residential streets sit beside shopping centers and restaurants. Water damage here usually comes from ordinary causes, such as a failed hose, a water heater or a roof leak, and it affects both houses and small businesses.

If you are standing in water now, stay clear of outlets, shut off the supply if it is safe and call (567) 406-9153.

Locating Westgate

A neighborhood guide places Westgate on the west side of Lima in ZIP code 45805, with U.S. Route 81 running through it. It lists Baxter Park as a small shaded park nearby and Faurot Park about a mile to the south.

We could not find an official boundary, so we describe Westgate by its landmarks. A shopping center anchored by a Walmart Supercenter lies along Route 81 just west of the residential blocks, according to the same guide.

Houses And Businesses Side By Side

The guide reports mostly mid-century houses, with a median build year of 1955, mixing ranches, foursquares and two-story homes on small lots with attached garages. Those figures are directory estimates, so we do not assume basement or crawl space details for any single address.

Along Route 81 there are stores, restaurants and offices. Commercial owners should read our commercial water damage restoration page, because a business loss is scoped around downtime and tenants.

Sewer Work And Heavy Rain

Lima settled a Clean Water Act case over sewage releases into the Ottawa River in 2014, and the EPA's summary describes new storage and sewer separation. The work targets overflows to the river. It does not guarantee that a particular basement drain will never back up.

If you have a floor drain, ask your insurer whether sewer backup is covered, since many standard policies leave it out.

Your First Hour

  1. Protect yourself first and stay out of water near electrical equipment.
  2. Stop the leak at the nearest shutoff if it is dry and reachable.
  3. Move small valuables and papers above the wet area.
  4. Take photographs and note the time you found the water.
  5. Call and describe what is wet.

A crew then starts with extraction and moisture mapping, and sets up drying equipment where readings show water in materials.
