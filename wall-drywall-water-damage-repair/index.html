Wall and Drywall Water Damage in Lima, OH

Drywall wicks water up and hides it behind paint. Learn how wet walls are found, dried or replaced.

Wall and drywall water damage repair is the process of finding wet wall cavities, drying them or cutting out wet drywall and rebuilding the finished surface. In Lima homes, water enters walls from plumbing, roofs, windows and floors, and drywall wicks it upward from the base. The wall can look fine while the cavity behind it stays wet.

Look for stained paint, soft spots, bubbling or a musty smell near the base of the wall. Turn off the water source if you can, and avoid drilling into a wet wall near wiring. Photograph the wall before anything is opened.

How Water Moves Through Walls

Wicking

Drywall draws water upward from the floor, often a foot or more above the visible wet line.

Plumbing in walls

Pinhole leaks and loose fittings soak cavities for weeks.

Roof and window leaks

Water runs down inside the wall from above.

Insulation

Wet insulation holds water against the drywall and framing.

Readings decide. A moisture meter on the drywall and the framing behind it shows what the wall can hold.

Repair Steps

  1. Stop the water source and map the wet area.
  2. Cut out wet drywall, usually along a clean line above the wet reading.
  3. Remove wet insulation and let the cavity open to air.
  4. Dry the framing with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  5. Hang new drywall, tape, mud and sand it.
  6. Prime and paint to match the existing finish.

Drying follows structural drying and dehumidification. Ceiling drywall has extra steps, covered on the ceiling water damage repair page.

Baseboards, Trim And Paint

Baseboards often swell when the wall wicks water, and they can usually be removed, dried and reinstalled if they are solid wood. MDF trim usually has to be replaced.

Paint matching depends on the age of the paint and the sheen. A full wall or room repaint is often needed to avoid visible patches.

Walls Behind Kitchens And Bathrooms

Walls near sinks, showers and appliances take repeated moisture. The kitchen water damage restoration page explains the kitchen version of this problem.

Tile and backer board in a bathroom need their own check, because water can sit behind them for a long time.

Odors And Mold Concerns

A musty smell from a wall means moisture is still present. Drying and removal of wet drywall address it. If you see mold, call a mold specialist, because mold remediation is not part of this site.

Documenting Wall Damage Before It Is Opened

Photograph the stain, the bubbling paint and any moisture meter readings before a wall is cut. Mark the edge of the stain with a pencil and a date so changes can be seen over a day or two.

Keep a piece of the removed drywall if your adjuster may want to see it, and ask the crew to note the highest wet reading on the written scope. That single number explains why the cut line is where it is.

Common Questions

Can wet drywall be dried?

Sometimes, if the water was clean and the drywall is still firm. Soft or crumbling drywall is replaced.

How far up do you cut?

Usually to a clean line above the highest wet reading, often higher than the visible stain.

Will the paint match?

Often a larger area or full wall needs to be repainted to blend.

Why does the wall still smell?

Moisture or wet insulation is likely still in the cavity.

Does insurance cover drywall repair?

Often for a sudden covered loss. Read your policy.
