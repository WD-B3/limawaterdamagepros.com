If Water Is Still Spreading In Spencerville

Stop the source

Close the shutoff at the fixture or the main valve where the line enters the house.

Protect the electrical

Stay away from wet outlets and the panel. Call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, electronics and papers above the wet line to dry rooms.

Photograph first

Take photos of the damage before cleanup so your insurer sees the full picture.

A Village Built Around A Canal

Spencerville was platted in 1844 along the Miami and Erie Canal, and the village sits at about 830 feet of elevation. A K-12 school building opened in 2008 and serves the village and surrounding township.

Older canal-era streets mean a mix of very old homes with long-lived plumbing and newer houses on the edges of the village. We do not claim that any particular street has more water problems, because we have no data to show it. What we can say is that older plumbing deserves a look when a leak turns up.

What Happens After You Call

  1. You describe the source, the rooms affected and whether the water is clean or contaminated.
  2. A crew comes out as soon as one is available and maps the wet areas with moisture meters.
  3. Standing water is removed and wet materials that cannot dry are taken out.
  4. Air movers and dehumidifiers run with daily readings until the structure is dry.
  5. You receive a written scope for repairs so you know the plan before work starts.

Insurance And Documentation

Sudden water damage is often covered by homeowners insurance, while long-term leaks and flood damage often are not. Check your policy or ask your agent what applies to your address.

Photographs, a list of damaged items and the moisture readings we take each day make a claim easier to document. Keep receipts for emergency steps you take.
