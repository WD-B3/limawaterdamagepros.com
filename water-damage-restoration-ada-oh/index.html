Water damage in Ada needs a quick response, and the 24/7 emergency line is open to Ada homes, rentals and businesses. Ada is in Hardin County, about 18 miles east of Lima, with 5,334 residents in the 2020 Census. Ohio Northern University was founded here in 1871.

If you have water in a basement or rental unit, stop the source if you can, keep out of standing water near electrical equipment and call. Photograph the damage before you begin cleaning.

Ada And The June 2015 Flooding

Local reporting in June 2015 described flooding in Ada after four to six inches of rain from the leading edge of Tropical Storm Bill. Reports mentioned basements taking on water and an overflowing retention pond at the university.

That kind of event is why we take basement calls seriously. See basement water damage cleanup for the process, and the flood damage restoration page for contaminated water.

Hog Creek And The Town Layout

Hog Creek runs along the north and east sides of Ada, and the village sits at about 958 feet of elevation. We do not claim that any particular block floods, but a creek nearby is a good reason to check your flood map and insurance.

The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows whether an address is in a mapped flood zone. Your agent can explain what a separate flood policy would cover.

Rentals And Student Housing

Ada has many rental homes because of the university. Ohio landlord law, in section 5321.04 of the Revised Code, requires landlords to keep plumbing in good working order and to make repairs that keep a unit habitable.

If you are a tenant and water appears, tell your landlord in writing, photograph the damage and keep copies. The blog guide on rental water damage explains the steps in more detail.

If Water Is Still Spreading In Ada

Call the landlord or owner

If you rent, report the leak right away and keep a record of the call.

Stop the flow

Close the valve at the fixture, or the main shutoff if you cannot reach it.

Protect belongings

Move electronics, books and papers above the wet line.

Take photos

Record the damage before anything is cleaned or removed.

Floors That Take Water

Carpet in apartments and student housing absorbs water quickly, and the pad underneath holds it. The carpet water damage restoration page explains when carpet can be dried and when it has to go.

Wood floors in older Ada homes follow the rules on the hardwood floor water damage repair page.

Other Communities Near Ada

We also respond to Alger, Forest and Arlington. Ada is between Lima and the towns to the east, and our crews dispatch from Lima. If you are heading west, Wapakoneta and Ottawa have their own pages.

What A Written Scope Tells You

Whether you own or rent, a written scope shows which rooms are wet, which materials are coming out and which equipment is being placed. It is the document that lets a landlord, a student family or an insurer agree on the facts.

Ask for the daily moisture readings too. They show when drying is complete, and they protect everyone if a dispute comes up later about whether the work was finished. Our AC leak water damage page shows a typical slow-leak case that the readings help prove.
