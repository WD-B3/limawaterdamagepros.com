Southside Lima is the south side of the city, near St. Johns Avenue and the parks along its edge. A water loss here can affect an owner living in the house or a landlord with a tenant, and both need a clear record of what was wet and what was done.

If water is coming in now, stop it if you can safely reach the valve, keep out of water near electrical equipment and call (567) 406-9153.

What Southside Means In Lima

A neighborhood guide places Southside Lima in ZIP code 45804, about a mile and a half from downtown, with St. Johns Avenue linking it to the center of town. It names Cook Park to the north and Foundry Park to the south, and it notes the area was once home to the Lima Locomotive Works.

The guide does not define exact boundaries and neither do we. We use the name for the south side of Lima and serve the whole city.

Owner-Occupied Houses And Rentals

The same guide reports a high share of renters and older houses from roughly 1900 to 1920 with some from the 1960s and 1970s. Those are directory descriptions and not a count.

Landlords and tenants should keep written records of any leak and who was told. Under Ohio law, section 5321.04 of the Revised Code, landlords must keep plumbing in good working order and make repairs needed to keep a unit fit and habitable.

Flood And Storm Context

Local reporting has described flooding in Lima after heavy rain, including water collecting at the Metcalf Street railroad underpass. We have no verified storm or flood record for Southside streets, and we do not say the area is flood-prone.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. A property-level answer comes from the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, not from a neighborhood name.

After The Water Is Out

Drying is measured, not guessed. Crews take readings in walls and floors each day, and equipment comes out when readings match dry reference points.

Ask for the written scope and readings, and keep them with your insurance papers. Your insurer decides coverage, and the answer depends on the cause and your policy.

Questions About Southside

I rent in Southside. Who calls you?

Tell the landlord in writing first. Landlords usually control the repair, and we can document the loss for both sides.

Does the Locomotive Works history affect water damage?

We have no evidence that it does. It is part of the area's history and nothing more here.

Is Southside prone to flooding?

We have no verified neighborhood flood records, and we do not claim it is.

How soon should I call?

As soon as you find water. Wet drywall and padding begin to break down within a day or two.

Do you answer at night?

Yes. Call (567) 406-9153. The line is answered 24/7.
