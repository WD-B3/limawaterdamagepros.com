A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs under or through a concrete slab floor, and the water damage it causes in Lima properties can spread beneath flooring long before it shows. The 24/7 emergency line is open if water is reaching the surface. This page covers the symptoms, the plumber and restoration roles, and the drying that follows.

If you suspect a slab leak, close the main water valve and watch the meter. If the dial keeps moving with everything off, water is escaping somewhere. Photograph any wet flooring, and call a plumber to locate the leak before drying starts.

Warm Floor Spots, A Rising Water Bill Or A Sound Under The Floor?

Most people never hear the term slab leak until a plumber says it. What you notice is a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water when nothing is on, a water bill that keeps climbing, or damp carpet at an exterior wall.

Those symptoms all point to a supply line leaking beneath the slab. Slab leak restoration is the drying, cleanup and flooring repair that follows once the line is found and repaired.

Does My Home Have A Slab Leak Risk?

Slab leaks apply to homes where supply lines run through or beneath concrete, including slab-on-grade houses, garages, additions and some finished basement floors. If your home has a basement or crawl space with accessible lines, a leak is more likely to be visible and easier to reach.

How common slab-on-grade construction is in Lima varies by neighborhood, and the county assessor and your own inspection report are the best sources for your property. We write the page around how the leak works, not around a frequency we cannot show.

How The Leak Is Found

Finding the leak is a plumber's job. Plumbers use acoustic and thermal tools to find where water escapes under the slab.

Restoration begins when the leak is located and repaired. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the wet area, and we dry the flooring and the walls near the leak.

What Water Does To Floors And Walls

Carpet and pad

Moisture rises into the pad from below and can stay unnoticed for weeks.

Hardwood and laminate

Boards cup or lift as moisture enters from beneath.

Baseboards and drywall

Water wicks into the base of the wall and discolors paint.

Concrete

The slab itself holds water and releases it slowly, so drying can take longer.

Repair Routes And Trade-Offs

A plumber may repair the line in place by breaking the slab, or reroute the supply overhead through walls and ceilings. Each choice affects how much finished floor must be removed and when drying can start.

Spot repairs are quicker but may leave the aging line to fail again, while rerouting is a bigger project. Ask your plumber for options before flooring comes up.

Drying After A Slab Leak

Drying focuses on the flooring above the leak and the wall bases around it, using air movers, dehumidifiers and, in some cases, floor drying systems that pull moisture from below. Daily readings tell when it is safe to rebuild.

The process mirrors the steps on the structural drying and dehumidification page. If flooring has cupped, see hardwood floor water damage repair for what can be saved.

What Affects The Cost Of Slab Leak Water Damage Cleanup

Cleanup costs are separate from the plumber's repair. The scope depends on the following.

Time the leak ran

Slow leaks that ran for weeks wet more flooring.

Flooring type

Tile can often stay, while wood and carpet may need replacing.

Area under the leak

Larger wet zones mean more equipment.

Plumber repair method

Breaking the slab creates extra dust and repair.

Rebuild

Finished flooring, baseboards and paint come after drying.

Many policies cover the resulting water damage but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself, and some exclude slab leaks or continuous seepage. Ask your insurer before work begins.

Common Questions

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Lima?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7. If water is reaching the surface, shut the main valve and call.

Can you find the slab leak?

A plumber locates the leak. We map moisture, dry the structure and repair finished surfaces.

Will the concrete need to be broken?

That is the plumber's decision and depends on the repair method chosen.

How do I know water is under my floor?

Warm spots, hissing sounds, damp carpet and a high water bill are common signs. A moisture meter helps confirm.

Is a slab leak covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Read your policy and ask your insurer which parts apply.
