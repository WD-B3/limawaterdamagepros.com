Sewage backup is an emergency, and the 24/7 emergency line is open for these calls in Lima. Sewage backup cleanup means removing contaminated water, taking out materials that cannot be cleaned, disinfecting what remains and drying the structure. The water is Category 3, which means it is grossly contaminated and unsafe to handle without protective gear.

Stop using sinks, toilets, showers and washers, because every flush or drain adds to the backup. Keep children and pets out of the area, avoid touching the water and do not run a household vacuum on it. Photograph the damage from the doorway and call.

When Sewage Backup Is An Emergency

Any sewage on a floor is an emergency, whether it came up a basement floor drain or out of a toilet. The longer contaminated water sits, the deeper it soaks into carpet, drywall and wood.

Same-day and after-hours response is offered when crews are available. Until the crew arrives, leave the area closed off and keep the household out of it.

Why Sewage Is Handled Differently

Category 3 water

Sewage carries bacteria and other contaminants, so it follows stricter procedures than clean water.

Porous materials

Carpet, pad, insulation and drywall that touched sewage are usually removed, not dried.

Protective gear

Crews wear gloves, boots, eye protection and respirators when working in contaminated areas.

Disinfecting

Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and treated after the bulk of the material is removed.

Sewage Backup Cleanup Steps

  1. Set up containment and make the area safe.
  2. Extract the contaminated water.
  3. Remove carpet, pad and wet porous materials and bag them for disposal.
  4. Clean and disinfect floors, framing and remaining surfaces.
  5. Dry the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  6. Confirm dry readings before any rebuild begins.

The extraction stage uses the same pumps described on the water extraction page, and the drying stage follows structural drying and dehumidification.

Why Backups Happen

Backups come from three places. A blocked line on your own property is the most common, and a plumber can clear it. A blocked or overloaded municipal main can push sewage back toward the house. Heavy rain can also overload a combined system.

In much of Lima, rain runoff and household waste travel in the same pipe. The EPA's summary of the city's 2014 Clean Water Act settlement describes new storage, including a 13 million gallon tank, and sewer separation meant to cut overflows to the Ottawa River.

This site does not claim that any particular neighborhood is more likely to back up. If you have a repeat problem, ask a plumber about a backwater valve and ask your insurer about sewer backup coverage.

What Insurance Often Says About Sewer Backup

Standard homeowners policies often exclude sewer or drain backup unless an endorsement was added. If you have the endorsement, coverage limits are often much lower than for other losses.

Read your declarations page and call your agent. Ohio's insurance department also runs a consumer services office at 800-686-1526 for general policy questions.

Standards Worth Knowing

Category 3 handling follows the water categories in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and the EPA also publishes guidance on cleaning up after sewage and flooding.

What Affects The Cost Of Sewage Backup Cleanup

Sewage jobs cost more than clean water jobs because of the safety work. These factors set the scope.

Area and depth

Rooms covered and the depth of the water decide how much material comes out.

Materials removed

Carpet, pad and drywall that must be discarded add labor and disposal.

Disinfecting

Cleaning and treating surfaces is a separate step from drying.

Drying

The structure still has to be dried after removal.

Rebuild

Replacing flooring, drywall and trim is separate from cleanup.

Common Questions

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Lima?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Stay out of the area until help arrives.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

It is not advised. Sewage carries contaminants and soaks into porous materials. Professional crews use protective gear and procedures designed for it.

Why is my carpet being thrown out?

Carpet and padding absorb contaminated water and cannot be reliably cleaned afterward.

Who fixes the clog?

A plumber clears the line. We handle the contaminated water, removal and drying.

Does insurance cover sewage backup?

Many policies exclude it unless an endorsement was added. Read your policy and ask your agent.
