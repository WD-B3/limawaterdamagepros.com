Northside Lima is the northern part of the city, where ranch-style houses sit on wider lots and farmland lies beyond. Most water damage calls from houses like these come from plumbing and appliances. This page explains the response and what to have ready when you phone.

Keep out of standing water near outlets and cords, shut off the source if you can reach it safely, and call (567) 406-9153.

Where Northside Is

A neighborhood guide places Northside Lima in ZIP codes 45801 and 45807, with Robb Park touching its southern end and restaurants along Route 65 on the east. It describes the area as surrounded by open farmland, with a Walmart Supercenter and a Lowe's toward Westgate along American Avenue.

There is no official boundary in the sources we found, so we describe the area by landmarks. Robb Park, a 26 acre park, is a useful reference point.

Ranch Houses And Slab Questions

The guide reports that ranch-style homes are the most common, with a median build year of 1961 and lots from a fraction of an acre up to about a quarter acre. Those are directory descriptions.

Ranch houses may or may not have basements, and we have no verified data on which Northside homes have them. If your home sits on a slab, a leak in a line under it can wet flooring before it shows. The slab leak water damage page explains the signs.

Appliances, Water Heaters And Winter

Laundry and dishwasher hoses

A failed hose can release water for hours. Read appliance leak water damage.

Water heaters

Tank failures release many gallons. See water heater leak cleanup.

Winter freezes

Lima's average January low is near 19 degrees Fahrenheit. A thaw can turn a frozen line into a wet house.

Storm water

Wind-driven rain and roof leaks are covered on storm water damage cleanup.

Flood And Drainage Facts We Can And Cannot Support

The neighborhood guide says residents do not struggle with flooding, but that is a general statement and not a verified record, so we do not repeat it as a fact. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified.

Heavy rain can still send water into a house through a roof, a window or a foundation wall. Only the FEMA Flood Map Service Center can answer for a specific address.

What The Cleanup Looks Like

  1. Water is extracted from carpet, flooring and low areas.
  2. Moisture meters and thermal imaging map the wet materials.
  3. Wet padding, insulation or drywall that cannot dry is removed.
  4. Air movers and dehumidifiers run with daily readings.
  5. A written scope covers the rebuild.

Ask to see the readings, since they decide when drying ends and are part of your insurance record.

Questions About Northside

Do ranch homes on slabs get water damage?

They can. Supply line leaks under a slab and appliance failures are common examples. We check readings before we assume anything.

Is Northside flood prone?

We have no verified neighborhood flood record. The FEMA status is not verified.

Do you serve Northside at night?

Yes. The emergency line is answered 24/7.

Can I dry a wet carpet myself?

A small clean-water spill, often. A larger one needs extraction and readings.

Will insurance pay?

Coverage depends on the cause and your policy. Ask your insurer.
