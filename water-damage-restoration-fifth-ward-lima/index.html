The Fifth Ward is one of Lima's city wards, and local news has covered a Walking the Wards meeting held for Fifth Ward residents. This page is for homeowners and landlords there who have found water and need to know what happens next.

If water is spreading right now, stop it at the source if you can reach the valve safely, stay out of any water near outlets or appliances and call (567) 406-9153. Tell us what is wet and whether the water looks clean or dirty.

Where The Fifth Ward Sits

A real estate neighborhood guide describes the Fifth Ward as running from the Ottawa River toward the Rose Avenue rail yards, with Industry Park to the north and the Lima refinery to the south. The same guide places downtown about a mile away and lists Hover Park, Collett Street Park and Simmons Field nearby.

The city does not publish neat boundaries for the ward in the sources we reviewed, so treat those edges as approximate. If your address is near the border of another ward, call and we will confirm we cover it. We serve all of Lima.

What We Know About The Housing

The same guide reports a median build year around 1920 and lists bungalows, Dutch Colonials, Cape Cods and some ranches. That is a directory estimate and not a survey, so we do not assume anything about the plumbing inside a particular house.

We also have no verified data on how many Fifth Ward homes have basements or crawl spaces. When you call, we ask which kind of foundation you have, because that changes where water collects and how drying equipment is placed.

Water Problems That Fit A River And Rail Corridor

Heavy rain and drains

Lima public works has cleared catch basins and storm drains after heavy rain, and city officials have asked residents to keep nearby catch basins clear. Water that cannot drain can reach low points around a house.

Plumbing failures

Supply lines, water heaters and washer hoses leak in homes of every age. The burst pipe water damage page explains what to shut off first.

Sewer or drain backup

Backups are Category 3 water. See sewage backup cleanup before you touch anything.

River and flood water

We have no verified flood record for individual Fifth Ward streets. If water came in from outside, follow the steps on flood damage restoration.

Flood Maps And What They Do Not Tell You

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified for the Fifth Ward. Only a property-level check can tell you whether your address is in a mapped flood hazard area, and the FEMA Flood Map Service Center is where to look it up.

A parcel outside a mapped zone can still flood, and a parcel inside one can stay dry for years. Mapped status mainly matters for flood insurance, which is separate from a standard homeowners policy.

Safe First Steps Before A Crew Arrives

  1. Keep people and pets out of water that may touch outlets, cords or appliances.
  2. Shut off the water supply only if the valve is dry and easy to reach.
  3. Do not run household vacuums or fans in standing water.
  4. Photograph the damage from a dry spot.
  5. Lift valuables off wet floors only if the area is safe to enter.
  6. Call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas danger.

If the water came from a toilet or a floor drain, treat it as contaminated and leave the area closed off.

Extraction, Drying And Records

Crews remove standing water first, as described on our water extraction page. Air movers and dehumidifiers then run with daily moisture readings until the structure reads dry.

Keep your photographs, the written scope and the readings together. They document the work, and your insurer decides what the policy covers. Ask your insurer what applies to your cause of loss before you decide on repairs.

Questions About Fifth Ward

Do you cover the whole Fifth Ward?

We serve all of Lima. The ward has approximate edges in the sources we found, so give us your address when you call and we will confirm.

Is the Fifth Ward in a flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer about flood coverage.

Are older homes more likely to leak?

Age alone does not tell us that. We look at the plumbing and the moisture readings in your specific house.

What should I do if a basement drain is backing up?

Stop using water, keep everyone out of the area and call. Backup water is contaminated.
