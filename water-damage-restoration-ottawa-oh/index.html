Water damage in Ottawa is an emergency, and the 24/7 emergency line is open for Ottawa homes and businesses. Ottawa is the county seat of Putnam County, about 22 miles north of Lima, with 4,456 residents in the 2020 Census. The Blanchard River runs through town.

If water is rising from outside or coming up through drains, keep out of the water, stay away from electrical equipment and call. Do not wait for the water to recede before you call.

The August 2007 Flood

The National Weather Service Northern Indiana office documented the August 2007 flood in Ottawa. Rainfall of 8 to 15 inches fell across Putnam and Van Wert Counties, and the Blanchard River crested near 31 feet against a flood stage of 23 feet. Downtown water depths reached 4 to 8 feet.

The NWS report notes it was the second highest crest on record, behind March 1913 at 33.3 feet. We use the event only to show what a major flood does, not to predict future ones. Cleanup after contaminated floodwater follows the flood damage restoration steps.

A Putnam County Town On The River

Ottawa is home to the Putnam County Airport and sits on the Blanchard River. The Ottawa River is a tributary of the Auglaize, and Lima also sits on a river of that name, so do not confuse the two.

Flood insurance is separate from homeowners insurance. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows whether your address is in a mapped flood zone.

When Water Is Still Rising

Move to higher ground

Do not wade through floodwater. It can hide debris and electrical hazards.

Call the utilities

Report downed lines or gas smells to the utility or 911.

Lift valuables

Move papers, electronics and keepsakes to upper floors.

Photograph the high-water line

Record it before the water drops and cleanup begins.

Pumping out the building follows the methods on the water extraction page.

What We Do After The River Recedes

  1. Check safety and shut off utilities where needed.
  2. Remove silt, debris and standing water.
  3. Take out porous materials that soaked up contaminated water.
  4. Clean and disinfect surfaces and framing.
  5. Dry the structure and take daily readings.
  6. Document the scope for your insurer.

Insurance And Paperwork After A Flood

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood, and sewer or drain backup is often excluded too. Read your policy and ask your agent what applies.

Keep photos of the high-water line, a list of damaged items and the daily readings. These make a claim easier to document.

Businesses Along The Blanchard River

Shops, offices and public buildings in a river town face two risks at once: the building floods, and the business closes while it dries. The right plan stages work by area so part of the building can reopen sooner.

Ask your insurer whether your commercial policy covers flood or business interruption, because many do not by default. Keep inventory lists, photographs and receipts together so claims move faster.
