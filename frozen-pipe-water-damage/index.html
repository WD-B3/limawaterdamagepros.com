Frozen pipe water damage often shows up hours after the cold ends, when ice melts and a split in the line starts releasing water. If you find a frozen pipe or a sudden drop in water pressure in Lima, treat it as an emergency, because the damage begins when the pipe thaws. The 24/7 emergency line is open for freeze calls.

Locate the main shut-off valve and close it before the pipe thaws. Open the faucet served by the frozen line so water has a path out as the ice melts. Never use an open flame to thaw a pipe, and keep power off to wet areas unless the panel is dry.

When A Frozen Pipe Becomes A Flood

Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split a pipe long before the ice melts. The split is often invisible until the line thaws, when water begins to pour through it.

That is why a stuck faucet on a cold morning deserves attention even if nothing is leaking yet. Shutting the main valve first removes the pressure that would push water through any hidden crack.

Where Pipes Freeze In Lima Homes

The 1991 to 2020 climate normals put the average January low in Lima near 19 degrees Fahrenheit, so freezing weather is a normal part of winter here. Lines in unheated garages, crawl spaces, outside walls and under sinks on exterior walls are the usual victims.

Exterior walls

Lines with thin insulation between them and the outside are first to freeze.

Unheated garages

Supply lines to laundry areas and utility sinks often run through cold garages.

Crawl spaces

Vents and gaps let cold air reach pipes under the floor. See crawl space water damage.

Vacant rooms

Closed-off areas with the heat turned down can fall below freezing overnight.

What To Do Before It Thaws

These steps lower the damage when a frozen line is found.

  1. Close the main water valve.
  2. Open the faucet nearest the frozen section.
  3. Warm the area gently with a space heater placed on a dry surface, never an open flame.
  4. Photograph the pipe and surrounding walls before they thaw.
  5. Call us before thawing if you cannot reach the shut-off, so a crew can head out.

Freeze Damage Cleanup

After a thaw, the first job is extracting standing water, which follows the steps on our water extraction page. Then moisture mapping shows how far the water traveled inside ceilings and walls.

Freeze breaks inside walls often wet insulation, which holds water for days. The burst pipe water damage page explains how wall cavities and ceilings below the break are opened, dried and repaired.

Preventing A Repeat

Insulating exposed lines, sealing air leaks around pipes and keeping the heat on when you are away are the main defenses. A plumber can reroute or insulate lines that freeze repeatedly.

Document any prevention steps you take. Insurers sometimes ask whether the property was heated and maintained when a freeze loss is reported.

Common Questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Lima?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, including cold nights and holiday weekends, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Only after shutting off the main valve, and only with gentle heat such as a hair dryer or space heater placed safely. Never use an open flame or a torch.

How do I know a pipe has already split?

Water pressure that returns weakly, a drip from a wall, or a stain on a ceiling after a thaw are common signs. A moisture meter can confirm it behind finishes.

Is a frozen pipe covered by insurance?

Often the resulting water damage is covered as sudden loss, but terms vary. Read your policy and ask whether heating requirements apply.

Can pipes freeze even if the heat stays on?

Yes. Lines in exterior walls or cold garages can freeze even in a heated house, especially during extended cold snaps.
