Flood damage in Lima is an emergency from the first inch, and the 24/7 emergency line is open for flood calls. Flood damage restoration is the removal of river, groundwater or surface water from a building, followed by cleaning, disinfecting, drying and repair. Because floodwater carries soil and contamination, it is handled very differently from a plumbing leak.

Do not wade through floodwater if you can avoid it. Water that has reached outlets, appliances or the panel can energize the area, so stay out and call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk first. Photograph the high-water line, lift valuables above it and wait for a crew before moving contaminated materials.

When Floodwater Is An Emergency

Floodwater is a safety issue before it is a property issue. It can hide sharp debris, carry sewage and make floors slippery, and it is never clean enough to wipe up with towels.

A flood that is still rising, covers a floor to the baseboards or has entered a finished basement should be treated as an emergency. After-hours and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

What Makes Flood Damage Different

Category 2 and 3 water

Floodwater is usually Category 3, which is grossly contaminated. Porous materials that soaked it up are usually removed, not dried.

Silt and debris

Mud and silt are carried inside and left behind. They must be removed before drying can work.

Longer drying

Cleaning comes before drying, and the structure often needs more days of monitored equipment time.

Contents

Soaked furniture, papers and electronics need sorting. See our guide on saving photos and documents in the blog.

Flood Cleanup Steps

  1. Make the area safe, including electrical and gas.
  2. Remove debris, mud and standing water with pumps and extraction units.
  3. Take out porous materials that held contaminated water, such as carpet, pad and wet drywall.
  4. Clean and disinfect hard surfaces and framing.
  5. Dry the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers, with daily moisture readings.
  6. Rebuild once readings are dry.

The first extraction step uses the same equipment described on our water extraction page, and the drying phase follows the process on structural drying and dehumidification.

Flood Insurance And Your Policy

Standard homeowners insurance usually excludes flood damage, and flood coverage typically has to be purchased separately. If you are not sure whether you have it, check your policy and ask your agent. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows whether your address sits in a mapped flood zone.

Sewer or drain backup is also often excluded unless an endorsement was added. Ask your insurer both questions before you make repair decisions.

What Affects The Cost Of Flood Cleanup

Flood jobs are usually larger and slower than leak jobs, and the factors below explain why.

Contamination level

Category 3 water requires removal of porous materials, cleaning and disinfecting.

Depth and area

The waterline height and the number of rooms affected set the scope.

Silt and debris

Mud removal adds labor before drying even begins.

Materials affected

Finished basements with carpet and drywall cost more to restore than bare concrete.

Drying and rebuild

Contaminated losses often need longer drying and more reconstruction.

Flood damage is usually not covered by a standard homeowners policy, so the cost conversation often involves flood insurance or your own funds. Read your declarations page and ask your agent about flood coverage and sewer backup.

Common Questions

Do you handle flood damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Lima?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Stay out of floodwater until the electrical and gas risks are cleared.

Is floodwater safe to clean up myself?

Floodwater can carry sewage, chemicals and debris. If you must enter, wear boots, gloves and eye protection, and keep children and pets out. A professional crew has the equipment and procedures for contaminated water.

Why is carpet thrown out after a flood?

Padding and carpet absorb contaminated water and are very hard to clean. Most are removed and replaced when Category 3 water was involved.

Does homeowners insurance pay for flood damage?

Standard policies usually exclude flood, and separate flood insurance is needed. Read your policy and ask your agent what applies to your address.

How long until I can return to my home?

It depends on safety, power and the extent of the damage. The crew will tell you once the area is safe and the plan is clear.
