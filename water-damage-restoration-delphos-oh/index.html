Water damage in Delphos is an emergency, and the 24/7 emergency line is open to Delphos homes and businesses. Delphos is about 15 miles northwest of Lima, with 7,117 residents in the 2020 Census. The city sits in both Allen and Van Wert Counties, which affects which county offices handle which records.

If water is rising or a drain is backing up, keep out of the water, stop using plumbing fixtures and call. We will ask what is wet and whether the water might be contaminated.

Water Damage Emergency In Delphos? Start Here

Speed and safety matter in the first hours. Standing water, sewage and water near electrical equipment each call for different first steps.

Clean water from a broken line is simplest. Contaminated water from a drain follows the steps on the sewage backup cleanup page, and flooding follows the flood damage restoration steps.

A City Formed From Four Towns

Delphos was formed in 1851 from four towns and sits on the Miami and Erie Canal. Its National Register listings include the Marks-Family House, St. John's Catholic Church and the Bredeick-Lang House, and the Auglaize River runs through the area.

Older homes and churches bring long-standing plumbing and roofs. If you manage an older building, our commercial water damage restoration page explains how larger losses are scoped.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Stop using drains

Do not flush toilets or run washers if drains are backing up.

Keep out of standing water

Water that touched sewage or flooded from outside may be contaminated.

Shut off power to wet areas

Only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. Otherwise call the utility.

Document the loss

Photograph the water and the damage from the dry side.

Two Counties, One Call

Delphos spans Allen and Van Wert Counties. For a repair that needs a building permit, ask the city or the county that covers your lot which office applies. We do not guess at the permitting authority for a particular address.

Our response does not change with the county line. The same 24/7 line serves both sides of town.

Typical Delphos Calls

Basement flooding

Heavy rain and high groundwater can bring water through walls and floors.

Sewer backups

A blocked line or an overloaded drain can push sewage into a basement.

Frozen pipes

Cold snaps hit exterior walls and garages first.

Roof leaks

Roof water shows up as ceiling damage, which is covered under ceiling water damage repair.

After The Water Is Out

Drying and repair come next. The crew checks moisture daily and removes equipment only when readings match dry. Repair then replaces what could not be saved.

For neighbors nearby, we also serve Ottawa and Spencerville, and the communities of Fort Jennings and Ohio City.

Sorting Contents After A Loss

Papers, photographs and electronics are often the hardest losses to accept. Lay wet papers flat in a cool place, keep photographs apart so they do not stick, and leave wet electronics unplugged until a technician checks them.

Furniture with hard surfaces can usually be cleaned, while upholstered pieces that touched contaminated water are often discarded. A written list of what was wet, with photographs, helps both the crew and your insurer decide what to save.
