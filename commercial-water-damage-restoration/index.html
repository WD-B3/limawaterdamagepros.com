Commercial water damage restoration covers water losses in shops, offices, restaurants, clinics, warehouses and multi-unit buildings. A commercial loss in Lima is an emergency for two reasons: the building is damaged, and the business is closed or disrupted while it dries. The 24/7 emergency line is open for commercial calls.

If you manage a building, stop the source if you can, shut off power to wet areas and move inventory and records out of the water. Photograph the area and notify your property manager or insurer. Then call so a crew can map the loss.

When A Commercial Loss Is An Emergency

A business cannot wait a day for a plan. Water in a retail space, an office suite or a restaurant kitchen affects customers, staff and health compliance within hours.

Same-day and after-hours response is offered when crews are available, including nights, weekends and holidays.

How A Commercial Job Differs

Downtime

The plan is built around reopening, so work can be staged by area.

Multiple tenants

Water often crosses unit lines, so scope and responsibility must be documented.

Larger footprints

More square footage means more equipment and a longer moisture map.

Records and inventory

Documents, stock and equipment need sorting and protection.

Commercial Restoration Process

  1. Stop the water and make the building safe.
  2. Map the loss across rooms, floors and tenant spaces.
  3. Extract water and remove wet materials such as ceiling tile and carpet.
  4. Dry with air movers and dehumidifiers using daily readings.
  5. Document the scope and readings for the owner, manager and insurer.
  6. Coordinate rebuild with the property manager.

The drying phase follows structural drying and dehumidification, and extraction uses the methods on the water extraction page.

Older Downtown Buildings

Older commercial blocks in Lima, such as the Golden Block downtown, were built with original plumbing runs and layered roofs. In a building like that, a small leak can travel through several floors and tenant spaces before anyone sees a stain.

A moisture map before cleanup is how we find the true extent of a loss. We do not claim anything about the condition of a specific building without seeing it.

Documentation For Owners And Insurers

Commercial claims move faster with clear records. We provide photographs, moisture maps, daily readings and an itemized scope, so owners, managers and insurers see the same facts.

Business interruption coverage, if you have it, is a separate coverage that your insurer explains. Ask your agent what your policy includes before the loss grows. Storefronts along Route 81 in Westgate are one example of the buildings we cover.

Standards Worth Knowing

Ask any commercial restorer whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, since a written standard gives owners and insurers a common yardstick.

What Affects The Cost Of Commercial Water Damage Restoration

Commercial scope is set by square footage and the building's use. These factors apply.

Square footage affected

More area means more equipment and labor.

Building materials

Ceiling tile, carpet tile and drywall behave differently.

Hours and access

Water category

Sewage and storm water require more safety steps.

Rebuild and downtime

Common Questions

Do you handle commercial water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Lima?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Can work be done while the business stays open?

Sometimes. Crews can stage work by area and contain wet zones, but it depends on the size and safety of the loss.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We document the scope and readings so managers, owners and insurers have the same information.

What about multi-unit buildings?

Water crossing unit lines is mapped room by room, so each space has its own scope.

Will you give an itemized scope?

Yes. The scope lists areas, materials and equipment before work begins.
